Privacy Policy

UpravlenieDomami Backup uses Google OAuth and the drive.file permission to create, read and manage authorized backup files in the account owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are used for authentication and must remain private.

Google Drive data is used solely for backup and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising or shared for unrelated purposes. Google and the configured backup tools process data as necessary to provide these functions. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Planned backup retention is one month; automatic retention is not yet configured. The account owner can delete backup files in Drive and revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections. Revocation does not itself delete stored files.

For privacy inquiries contact the developer through the repository's issue tracker without posting private data.
